Introduction

How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide and maintain the core teleprompter and overlay functionality.
  • Save your preferences and scripts locally across sessions.
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the app.
  • Identify and fix crashes or bugs to improve app stability.

We do not use your data for profiling, sell it to data brokers, or use it for any purpose unrelated to operating this app.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use certain data from your device — such as the Advertising ID — to show personalized or non-personalized ads.

Google's data collection and use practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy . You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by adjusting your device settings:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device.
  • Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (Android 12 and below), or reset / delete your Advertising ID (Android 13+).

We recommend reviewing Google's AdMob & Privacy guidance for more information.

App Permissions

Our app requests certain Android permissions that are necessary for its core functionality. Below is a plain-language explanation of each:

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to show the floating teleprompter overlay on top of your livestream or camera app. This is the core feature of the app. No screen content is recorded or transmitted.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Required to keep the overlay running reliably while you switch between apps or go live. The app shows a persistent notification while this service is active, as required by Android.
  • Internet access (INTERNET): Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob.
[VERIFY: Add or remove any other permissions your AndroidManifest.xml declares — e.g. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATE, etc.]
