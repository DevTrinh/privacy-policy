01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all Android applications published by DevTrinh ("we", "our", or "us") on Google Play.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not operate backend servers, require account registration, or collect personal data beyond what is described below.

By using any of our applications, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

02

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Content you create or configure within the app — stored locally on your device only.
  • App settings and preferences — stored locally on your device.

Information collected automatically:

  • Advertising data (where applicable): Our ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers such as your Advertising ID to serve relevant ads.
  • Crash & diagnostics data (where applicable): Some apps may use an anonymous crash reporting service. Reports may include device model, OS version, and a stack trace — no personally identifiable information is included.
We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, camera feed, or microphone audio.
03

How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of each application.
  • Save your preferences and content locally across sessions.
  • Display advertisements via Google AdMob to support free access to our apps.
  • Identify and fix crashes or bugs to improve app stability.

We do not use your data for profiling, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose unrelated to operating our apps.

04

Advertising

Some of our apps use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob is provided by Google LLC and may collect device data such as your Advertising ID to show personalized or non-personalized ads.

Google's practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device.
  • Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (Android 12 and below), or reset/delete your Advertising ID (Android 13+).
05

App Permissions

Each application requests only the Android permissions that are strictly necessary for its features to function. No sensitive permissions (such as camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location) are requested unless they are a core part of an app's functionality and are clearly disclosed on its Google Play listing.

The complete list of permissions for each app is visible on its Google Play store listing under Data safety and App permissions.
06

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share limited, non-personal data only in the following cases:

  • Google AdMob: Device signals shared for ad delivery as described in Section 4.
  • Crash reporting (where applicable): Anonymous diagnostic data may be shared with a crash reporting provider (e.g. Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or valid legal process.

Your personal content remains entirely on your device and is never shared with us or any third party.

07

Data Retention

All content and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Data held by third-party services (such as AdMob or a crash reporter) is subject to their respective retention policies.

08

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our apps. Since user content is stored locally, its security depends on your device's own security settings (e.g. screen lock, encryption).

We encourage you to keep your Android OS and our apps updated to benefit from the latest security improvements.

09

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal information through one of our apps, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
