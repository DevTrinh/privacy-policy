01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all Android applications published by DevTrinh ("we", "our", or "us") on Google Play. Each app is a local utility tool designed to run entirely on your device.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not operate our own backend servers, do not require account registration, and do not collect personal data beyond what is described in this policy.

By using any of our applications, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

02

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Content you create or import within the app (e.g. scripts, text) — stored locally on your device only.
  • App preferences and settings you configure — stored locally on your device.

Information collected automatically:

  • Advertising data: Our third-party ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect certain device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID) and usage signals to serve relevant ads. See Section 4 for details.
  • Crash & diagnostics data (where applicable): Some apps may use an anonymous crash reporting service. Reports may include device model, OS version, and a stack trace — no personally identifiable information is included.
We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, camera feed, microphone audio, or any other personal identifier unless explicitly described above.
03

How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of each application.
  • Save your preferences and content locally across sessions.
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to our apps.
  • Identify and fix crashes or bugs to improve app stability.

We do not use your data for profiling, sell it to data brokers, or use it for any purpose unrelated to operating our apps.

04

Advertising

Our apps use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use certain data from your device — such as the Advertising ID — to show personalized or non-personalized ads.

Google's data collection and use practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy . You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by adjusting your device settings:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device.
  • Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (Android 12 and below), or reset / delete your Advertising ID (Android 13+).

We recommend reviewing Google's AdMob & Privacy guidance for more information.

05

App Permissions

Each application requests only the Android permissions necessary for its specific features. Common permissions across our apps include:

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used by overlay-based apps to show a floating interface on top of other apps. No screen content is recorded or transmitted.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Keeps certain features running reliably while you switch between apps. A persistent notification is shown as required by Android while the service is active.
  • Internet access (INTERNET): Required to load advertisements from Google AdMob.
The full list of permissions for each app is visible on its Google Play store listing, under Data safety and App permissions.

We request only the permissions necessary to deliver each app's features. We never access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or location unless a specific app feature requires it and it is clearly disclosed on its store listing.

06

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share limited, non-personal data only in the following cases:

  • Google AdMob: As described in Section 4, AdMob receives device signals for ad delivery purposes.
  • Crash reporting (where applicable): Anonymous crash diagnostic data may be shared with a third-party service (e.g. Firebase Crashlytics) to help us fix bugs.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal processes.

Your personal content remains entirely on your device and is never shared with us or any third party.

07

Data Retention

Because your content and preferences are stored locally on your device, you control them directly. You can delete any stored data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Any data held by third-party services (such as AdMob or a crash reporter) is subject to their respective retention policies.

08

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information within our apps. Since all user content is stored locally on your device, the security of that data depends on your device's security settings (e.g., screen lock, encryption).

No method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your Android OS and our apps updated to benefit from the latest security patches.

09

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal information through one of our apps, please contact us and we will take prompt steps to delete it.
